About Auberge at North Ogden

Auberge at North Ogden is an 88-bed assisted-living and memory-care community at 1340 N Washington Blvd. in North Ogden, Utah. The dual licensing across assisted living and memory care puts the property in a small group of Weber County senior buildings that carry both designations, and the building was formerly known as Brookdale North Ogden before the rebrand to the Auberge name. The care model covers two sides of the building.

Sixty-eight beds sit on the assisted-living side, where staff handle bathing, dressing, grooming, medication management, and care coordination, while twenty beds make up the secured memory-care neighborhood with its own structured environment for dementia residents. On-site occupational, physical, and speech therapy services run through the building, which is uncommon at the assisted-living level and saves residents from outside appointments.

The Auberge brand's SPARK program shapes daily life across both wings. The Montessori-inspired approach focuses on purpose-driven activities, social inclusion, and meaningful connection, with music sessions, group exercise, arts and crafts, and structured activity blocks calibrated to each resident's cognitive and physical level. A professional culinary team prepares chef-crafted meals daily, and scheduled transportation handles medical appointments and resident outings.

Staff compassion through difficult dementia transitions, the cleanliness of the building, and the quality of chef-crafted meals shape the everyday rhythm here. Medicaid is accepted, which keeps the care accessible to Ogden-area families on a Medicaid budget, and the building is also pet-friendly. That combination of Medicaid acceptance with pets welcome is uncommon at the same community. North Ogden sits at the north end of Weber County, and the Washington Boulevard address puts Ogden Regional Medical Center about 10 minutes south for medical care.

The Newgate Mall and Ogden retail corridor are within a 10-minute drive for shopping and casual dining, the Wasatch Mountains rise immediately east for scenic family visits, and I-15 is accessible via 12th Street a few minutes west. The community runs under Frontier Management, LLC, a senior-living group that runs properties across the western United States under the Auberge brand. The combination of dual care designations, Medicaid acceptance, the pet-friendly policy, and the SPARK engagement model makes this building a notable stop in the Weber County senior-living set.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of care does Auberge at North Ogden offer?

Assisted living and memory care under one roof, with 68 beds on the assisted-living side and a separate secured 20-bed memory-care neighborhood. On-site occupational, physical, and speech therapy services support both wings.

How much does Auberge at North Ogden cost?

Pricing varies by care wing and bedroom layout. Both private-pay and Medicaid figures shift quarter to quarter, and the most current rate sheet is available through your advisor.

Does Auberge at North Ogden accept Medicaid?

Yes, Medicaid is accepted at this community, which keeps the dual assisted-living and memory-care services accessible to Weber County families on a Medicaid budget. Bed availability varies, so confirming current openings before the tour is wise.

Where is Auberge at North Ogden located?

1340 N. Washington Blvd. at the north end of Weber County. Ogden Regional Medical Center sits roughly ten minutes down Washington, and I-15 is reachable via 12th Street.

How many beds does Auberge at North Ogden have?

88 beds total, with 20 in the secured memory-care neighborhood and 68 on the assisted-living side. The dual-license setup allows transitions between care levels without a building change.

What dining is available?

A professional culinary team prepares chef-crafted meals daily in the main dining room. Snacks stay accessible between meals, and dietary accommodations are handled on request.

What activities and amenities are offered?

The SPARK Montessori-inspired program runs through music, group exercise, arts and crafts, bingo, and live entertainment. On-site OT, PT, and speech therapy services are uncommon at the assisted-living level.

Does Auberge at North Ogden allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome. Pairing Medicaid acceptance with a pet-friendly policy at the same community is unusual in northern Utah senior living, and many local families cite it as a deciding factor when choosing this property.

Who operates Auberge at North Ogden?

Frontier Management, LLC operates the property under the Auberge brand, which runs senior-living properties across the western United States. The community was previously known as Brookdale North Ogden before the Auberge rebrand.

How do I schedule a tour of Auberge at North Ogden?

Share your timeline through the contact form on this page. Your senior advisor calls back within a business day to coordinate the tour and walk through how the dual assisted-living and memory-care setup matches a specific resident's needs.
